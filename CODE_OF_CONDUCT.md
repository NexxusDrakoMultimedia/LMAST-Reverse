# Code of Conduct

These rules apply everywhere the project meets people: issues, pull
requests, commit messages, discussions, and anywhere else you speak for
or about LMAST-Reverse. They are based on the rules of the Discord
community of the maintainer, Nexxus (Pyra Drake).

## The rules

1. **Be excellent to each other, always.** If you have to ask yourself
   "am I acting a fool?", the answer is likely yes. This isn't a
   popularity contest, and if you're an asshat, no one will miss you.
2. **No bigotry.** Racism, sexism, transphobia, intersexphobia,
   homophobia, biphobia, acephobia and any other bigotry get you
   removed, no warnings and no debate. This project is queer-run and
   queer-made, and every queer voice is welcome and actively backed
   ([Acknowledgements](README.md#acknowledgements)).
3. **Don't be a creep.** Acting creepy, edgy or lewd towards anyone gets
   you excluded.
4. **No explicit or NSFW content**, in text, images or links.
5. **No illegal material.** That includes the game itself: never post
   or link disc images, extracted game files, or other copyrighted data
   (see [Ground rules](CONTRIBUTING.md#ground-rules)). Everyone works
   from their own copy of the disc.
6. **No political discussion.** It's divisive and has no place in a
   reverse-engineering project.
7. **No low-quality content.** Everything you post should have a point,
   and some care or labour put into it. Mass-generated AI output you
   haven't checked doesn't count. AI help is welcome here, but you are
   responsible for what you submit
   ([Using AI assistants](CONTRIBUTING.md#using-ai-assistants)).
8. **Cite your sources.** Say where a fact, tool or file came from, and
   credit the people who made it. In the docs, that means labelling a
   claim confirmed or empirical and giving its evidence.
9. **No login-walled links.** Link pages that anyone can read without
   an account. Requiring one is an act of bad faith against the open
   internet.
10. **Write in English.** The maintainers can only moderate English, and
    this project serves the English-speaking LMAST community. Quoting
    the game's own Japanese text, or other languages under study, is
    fine.
11. **Don't ask to speak, just speak.** You don't need permission to
    open an issue, ask a question or suggest an idea. If anyone gets
    the impression that they do, we've failed.

## Enforcement

This project is not a democracy. The maintainer may remove comments,
commits, issues or pull requests, and may block anyone from the project,
at their discretion and for any reason. Breaking a rule isn't required:
bending the rules, being annoying, or failing a basic vibe check is
enough.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately, through the
[LMAST/SFCC English Fan Discord](https://discord.gg/7DNrGye9TZ) or
GitHub ([NexxusDrakoMultimedia](https://github.com/NexxusDrakoMultimedia)).
Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer and the community can't help with a mental health or
other crisis. Please use [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com/i/iasp)
to find a local helpline, or if the crisis is life-threatening, contact
your local emergency services.
